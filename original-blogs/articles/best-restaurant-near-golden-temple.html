Amritsar is not just a city of faith — it’s a city of food. Every year, millions of visitors walk through the gates of the Golden Temple, and when hunger strikes after darshan, the search begins for the best restaurant near Golden Temple. The answer, for over a century, has always been the same: Kesar Da Dhaba.

Established in 1916, Kesar Da Dhaba is one of Amritsar’s oldest and most celebrated vegetarian restaurants. For generations, it has welcomed locals, pilgrims, and visitors seeking traditional Punjabi food prepared with the same commitment to flavour and quality that built its reputation over a century ago.

“For generations, Kesar Da Dhaba has focused on one thing above all else — serving traditional Punjabi food that keeps people coming back.”

A 110-Year Legacy That No Food App Algorithm Can Replicate

When you look up where to eat near the Golden Temple, you’ll find dozens of options — new cafes, multi-cuisine spots, and tourist-friendly eateries. But Kesar Da Dhaba predates all of them. For more than a century, it has welcomed locals and visitors alike, earning a reputation as one of Amritsar’s most iconic vegetarian dining destinations.

The founding family began with one vision: to feed people honest, home-style Punjabi vegetarian food. That vision has never changed. Today, the third and fourth generation of the same family run the dhaba, using recipes that have never been written down — because they’ve never needed to be. They’ve been passed from hand to hand, pot to pot, for over a century.

What to Order at Kesar Da Dhaba

100% Pure Vegetarian

If you’re visiting Amritsar and looking for the best restaurant near Golden Temple, here’s exactly what to order. Every dish on this list is a reason people come back.

  • Dal Fry: Slow-cooked lentils finished with ghee, cumin, and a signature tadka. Rich, comforting, and deeply satisfying — the dish that has become synonymous with Kesar Da Dhaba.
  • Channa Fry: Whole chickpeas slow-cooked in a dry, spiced masala until rich and slightly crispy on the edges. Drier and more intense than regular chole — far more satisfying.
  • Palak Paneer: Earthy, smoky, and far better than most versions you’ll find elsewhere. The earthen flavors of the kitchen come through in every spoonful.
  • Phirni: Served in traditional earthen bowls, this chilled rice pudding is the perfect sweet finish. Many visitors say this alone is worth the trip.
  • Lachha Parantha: Layered, buttery, golden crisp on the tawa. Every layer pulls apart beautifully and holds the dal and channa just right. The ideal companion.
  • Lassi: Thick, cold, and topped with a generous layer of malai. In Amritsar’s heat, this is non-negotiable. Order one before the food arrives.

* Pro tip: Kesar Da Dhaba is especially popular during weekends, holidays, and festive periods. Visiting slightly before peak lunch or dinner hours can help you enjoy a more relaxed dining experience.

Unpolished, Unpretentious, Unmissable

Kesar Da Dhaba’s charm lies in its authenticity. The focus here has always been on food, tradition, and hospitality, creating an atmosphere that feels distinctly Amritsari and wonderfully timeless.

The energy is warm, a little chaotic, and completely genuine. You’ll find yourself eating next to a family from Delhi, a solo traveller from abroad, and a local uncle who’s been eating here every Sunday for forty years. That mix of people — all united by the same plates of food — is something no upscale restaurant can manufacture.

“This is what makes Kesar Da Dhaba the best restaurant near Golden Temple — not just in terms of food, but in terms of experience.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you visit

Is Kesar Da Dhaba vegetarian?

Yes, Kesar Da Dhaba is 100% pure vegetarian. It has always been a veg-only restaurant — which is also in keeping with the sanctity of its location near the Golden Temple in Amritsar.

What is the most famous dish at Kesar Da Dhaba?

The dal fry is the most iconic dish here — slow-cooked with ghee and a classic tadka. The channa fry and lachha parantha are equally loved by regulars and are the perfect accompaniments.

How far is Kesar Da Dhaba from the Golden Temple?

Kesar Da Dhaba is located in Chowk Passian, Shastri Market — roughly a 10 to 15-minute walk from the main entrance of the Golden Temple. It’s one of the most well-known and accessible restaurants in the area.

What are the timings of Kesar Da Dhaba?

Kesar Da Dhaba is generally open from 12 PM to 11 PM. Timings may occasionally vary on special occasions.

Why is Kesar Da Dhaba considered the best restaurant near Golden Temple?

Kesar Da Dhaba has been serving authentic Punjabi vegetarian food since 1916 — over 110 years of consistent, unchanged recipes made by the same family. No other restaurant in the area comes close to that kind of heritage, trust, and depth of flavour.

Is Kesar Da Dhaba suitable for families?

Yes. Kesar Da Dhaba has been a favourite among families for generations. Its vegetarian menu and welcoming atmosphere make it a popular choice for visitors of all ages.

What should first-time visitors order at Kesar Da Dhaba?

First-time visitors should start with Dal Fry, Channa Fry, Lachha Parantha, and Lassi — then finish with the Phirni dessert for the complete experience.

Why is Kesar Da Dhaba famous?

Kesar Da Dhaba is known for its long-standing culinary heritage, traditional Punjabi vegetarian cuisine, and signature dishes that have attracted diners from across India and around the world for generations.
