When you visit Amritsar, one food place you simply cannot miss is Kesar Da Dhaba. Located in the old lanes near the Golden Temple, this iconic dhaba has been serving authentic Punjabi vegetarian food for more than 100 years. The food here is simple, rich, and full of desi flavours.

In this blog, we will explore the menu at Kesar Da Dhaba and describe the best dishes you must try when you visit.

Menu Description

The menu at Kesar Da Dhaba is simple and focused. You will not find a long list of hundreds of items. Instead, the dhaba serves selected traditional Punjabi dishes that they have perfected over the years.

The menu mainly includes:

  • Pranthas
  • Sabziyan
  • Thalis
  • Raita and Pulao
  • Rotiya
  • Desserts
  • Beverages

Everything is vegetarian. The dishes are prepared fresh, and most of them are cooked slowly to bring out deep flavours. Butter and desi ghee are used generously, which gives the food its signature rich taste.

The staff usually recommends their famous Dal Fry, Baingan Da Bartha, and Laccha Prantha. Many visitors also order Palak Paneer and end their meal with Firni or Tille Wali Kulfi.

Now let us look at each of the must-try dishes in detail.

Dal Fry

Dal Fry is the most famous dish at Kesar Da Dhaba. In fact, many people visit the dhaba only to eat this dal.

The dal is slow-cooked overnight on a low flame. This slow cooking process allows the flavours to develop deeply. The lentils become soft and creamy. After cooking, the dal is finished with a generous amount of desi ghee.

The texture is thick and smooth. The taste is rich but not too spicy. It has a comforting flavour that reminds you of home-cooked food. When served hot with butter melting on top, the aroma itself is enough to make you hungry.

Dal Fry tastes best when eaten with Laccha Prantha. The combination of creamy dal and layered paratha creates a perfect Punjabi meal.

Baingan Da Bartha

Baingan Da Bartha is another popular dish that reflects traditional Punjabi cooking.

The brinjal (baingan) is roasted on an open flame until the skin becomes charred. This roasting gives the dish a smoky flavour that makes it unique. After roasting, the brinjal is mashed and cooked with tomatoes, onions, green chilies, and traditional spices.

The texture is soft and slightly chunky. The smoky aroma blends beautifully with the spices. It is not overly spicy, but it has a deep, earthy flavour.

When eaten with Laccha Prantha or roti, Baingan Da Bartha feels like a meal made in a traditional Punjabi home. It is simple, authentic, and satisfying.

Palak Paneer

Palak Paneer is one of the most loved vegetarian dishes in Punjabi cuisine. At Kesar Da Dhaba, this dish is prepared in a traditional style that focuses on freshness and flavour.

The paneer used in the dish is soft and tender. It is cooked in a smooth spinach gravy made from fresh palak leaves. The spinach is blended into a creamy paste and then slow-cooked with onions, garlic, mild spices, and desi ghee.

The result is a thick, rich green gravy that tastes both fresh and comforting. The spices are balanced, so the natural flavour of spinach and paneer stands out clearly.

Laccha Prantha

No Punjabi meal is complete without a good prantha, and Laccha Prantha at Kesar Da Dhaba is truly special.

The paratha is made with multiple thin layers of dough. It is cooked in desi ghee until it becomes slightly crispy on the outside and soft on the inside. The layers separate beautifully when you tear it.

It has a light crispness and a rich buttery taste. Laccha Prantha pairs perfectly with Dal Fry, Baingan Da Bartha, and Palak Paneer.

Each bite gives you a mix of softness and crunch, making it one of the most satisfying parts of the meal.

Special Thali

If you cannot decide what to order, the Special Thali is the best option.

The thali usually includes Dal Fry, Palak Paneer,Chana Plain, Pranthas, and Raita. It gives you a complete experience of Punjabi food in one plate.

The thali is filling and balanced. It allows you to taste different flavours in one meal. Many first-time visitors choose the thali to experience the variety offered at the dhaba.

It is perfect for tourists who want a full traditional meal without ordering too many separate dishes.

Firni

No meal at Kesar Da Dhaba is complete without ending it with something sweet, and Firni is the perfect choice.

Firni is a traditional Punjabi dessert made from ground rice, milk, and sugar. It is slow-cooked until it becomes thick and creamy. The mixture is then poured into small clay bowls and chilled before serving.

The texture of Firni is smooth and rich, yet light on the stomach. It is mildly sweet, not overpowering, which makes it a refreshing dessert after a heavy Punjabi meal. It is garnished with chopped nuts like almonds or pistachios for extra flavour and crunch.

Served cold, Firni balances the richness of dishes like Dal Fry and Paneer. It is simple, traditional, and comforting, just like the food at Kesar Da Dhaba itself.

Tille Wali Kulfi

Tille Wali Kulfi is one of the most special desserts you can try at Kesar Da Dhaba.

“Tille” means sesame seeds. This kulfi is different from regular kulfi because it includes roasted sesame seeds, which give it a slightly nutty and rich flavour. The kulfi is made using thickened milk, sugar, and traditional slow-freezing methods.

The texture is dense, creamy, and smooth. It is not overly sweet, which makes it enjoyable even after a heavy Punjabi meal. The sesame adds a unique roasted taste that makes this dessert stand out.

Served chilled, Tille Wali Kulfi is the perfect way to end your meal. It adds a traditional Punjabi touch and leaves you with a sweet and memorable finish.

Conclusion

When you are in Amritsar, visiting Kesar Da Dhaba should be on your list. It is more than just a famous food spot. It is a place where tradition is still alive in every dish that is served.

As you sit down and look around, you will notice the simplicity of the space. The real focus is on the food. The Dal Fry is rich and creamy. The Laccha Prantha is soft with perfect layers. The Palak Paneer feels fresh and comforting. Each dish is prepared with care, using desi ghee and traditional cooking methods.

When your meal ends with Firni or Tille Wali Kulfi, you feel satisfied and content. The sweetness is balanced and refreshing after a hearty Punjabi meal.

Do not rush your visit. Take your time. Share the dishes with the people sitting next to you. Enjoy the warmth, the flavours, and the experience. At Kesar Da Dhaba, you are not just having lunch or dinner. You are enjoying a piece of Punjab’s food heritage that has been loved for generations.
